1. Executive Summary Strategic Mission & Operational Foundation
Campaign Goal
Attract, validate, and onboard 500 final-year engineering students into a free online workshop in 7 days, maintaining zero tolerance for spam or fabricated metrics.
Budget Discipline
Strict ₹2,000 total ceiling prioritizing organic community distribution. Capital is allocated as an optional test and defensive reserve; unspent capital is preferred over wasteful spend.
Execution Paradigm
Permission-first distribution through verified WhatsApp groups, Class Representatives, and student clubs, reinforced by an attribution-tracked peer referral engine.
2. Student Understanding & Audience Hypotheses Hypotheses to Validate (Not Concluded Research Findings)
Audience Dimension Working Behavioral Hypothesis Strategic Countermeasure on Landing Page & Messaging
Primary Motivation Final-year engineers urgently need tangible technical projects to discuss during upcoming campus placements and off-campus recruitment rounds. Positions a real, working project ("AI Text Summarizer in ~45m") as the primary takeaway rather than theoretical slides or passive lectures.
Friction: Difficulty Students fear AI requires advanced mathematics, neural network theory, or expensive GPU hardware setups they do not possess. Clear prerequisite copy: "No prior AI experience required. If you can write basic code in any language, you can build this."
Friction: Time Commitment Students juggle semester projects, lab exams, and placement training; standard 3-hour webinars face severe registration and attendance drop-offs. Explicit 60-minute timebox guarantee prominently highlighted in the hero headline, syllabus breakdown, and confirmation page.
Friction: Upsell Skepticism Students are saturated with "free masterclasses" that disguise aggressive sales pitches for expensive certificate courses. High-trust disclosure: 100% free, zero hidden fees, zero unsolicited WhatsApp spam, and open-source starter code provided upfront.

6. Measurement Framework & Decision Governance Truthful Denominators & Daily Reallocation Protocol
Telemetry & Guardrail Metrics
• Primary KPI: Verified registrations stored in SQLite against the 500-seat ceiling.
• Channel Contribution: Tracked via bySource aggregation against planned quotas.
• Conversion Rate (CR%): Calculated strictly when valid visit impression denominators exist.
• Cost Per Acquisition (CAC): Calculated strictly against actual disbursed ad spend.
Daily Reallocation Protocol
• Daily 18:00 IST Audit: Compare channel registration run-rates against the ~72/day baseline.
• Underperformance Rule: If a channel is >30% below pace by Day 4, outreach effort is reallocated to high-converting CRs.
• Zero Fabricated Metrics: No decorative counters or arbitrary multipliers.

8. Strategic Risks and Mitigations Defensive Risk Management
Limited Organic Reach & Admin Gatekeeping
Risk: WhatsApp group admins refuse promotional posts.
Mitigation: Pitch as non-commercial academic upskilling; provide co-branding recognition for participating student coding clubs.
Attribution Leakage & Direct Drift
Risk: Students strip UTM tags when forwarding links.
Mitigation: Pre-shorten links with baked-in parameters; utilize unique student referral codes as a persistent secondary attribution layer.
Landing Page Form Abandonment
Risk: Visitors bounce when encountering long registration fields.
Mitigation: Constrain form strictly to 5 essential academic fields; provide immediate visual confirmation without email verification barriers.
Spreadsheet Formula Injection Vulnerability
Risk: Malicious student input executes DDE commands in Excel exports.
Mitigation: Automatic sanitization prefixing formula triggers (=, +, -, @) with single quotes in the CSV export engine.
9. Pre-Registered Growth Experiments Planned Methodological Tests (Not Claimed as Completed)
EXP-01: Headline Framing
Project Outcome vs. Abstract Skill
Hypothesis: "Build an AI App in 60m" converts higher than "Learn AI Fundamentals" due to placement portfolio demand.
Metric: Landing Page CR% (min. 150 visits/variant).
Decision: Adopt if outcome variant ≥ 15% lift (p < 0.05).
EXP-02: Message Format
Finished Preview vs. Syllabus Bullets
Hypothesis: Visual card showing the finished Text Summarizer drives higher CTR in class groups than text-heavy syllabus points.
Metric: Tracked UTM link CTR & completed registrations.
Decision: Standardize if visual card ≥ 25% lift across 10 groups.
EXP-03: Referral Incentive
Study Partner vs. Generic Share
Hypothesis: "Invite a Project Partner to Build Together" yields higher viral factor than generic "Invite Friends".
Metric: Referral coefficient (k-factor = referral regs / initial regs).
Decision: Prioritize referral modal if k > 0.20 on Day 5.
